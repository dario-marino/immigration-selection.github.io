Select a mode above, set a threshold, then click any country on the map to see exactly which sex × education × age-at-arrival groups from that country clear the bar.
Sex × education × age-at-arrival groups computed from IPUMS USA / American Community Survey microdata (person-weighted). Income is mean total personal income (IPUMS INCTOT). Institutionalization is the share of each group's ACS group-quarters population recorded in group quarters, per 100,000, restricted to ages 60 and under to reduce the elderly-care/nursing-home skew — ACS does not release which type of institution someone is in (correctional, mental health, and other institutional care are bundled together on the public file), so this is a proxy for institutionalization broadly, not a crime rate specifically. Groups built from fewer than 30 sampled records are marked unreliable and never count toward a country's qualifying total. GDP per capita is PPP, most recent year available; countries below a set threshold are shown in the excluded color regardless of group counts. See the complete table for every group, every country, with reliability flags, or the visa category reallocation tool for a separate, DHS-sourced view of actual LPR admissions by category and country.
